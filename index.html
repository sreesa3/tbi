About TBI

Building startups from campus and community

The SCSVMV Technology Business Incubator is a joint venture of Sri Chandrasekharendra Saraswathi Viswa Mahavidyalaya and the Government of India. It was officially sanctioned by DST, Government of India on 19 July 2007 and functions as a society with autonomous status.

TBI helps create sustainable technology companies from innovative ideas. Students with entrepreneurial spirit, members of the public, and existing firms incubating spin-off ideas can use this platform for mentoring, infrastructure, and commercialization support.

How to apply

Ideas are screened for innovativeness, commercial viability, technical feasibility, social sensibility, and scalability.

Selection criteria

  • Applications via website and media announcements
  • Informal interview on the proposed project
  • Significance and homework done by the incubate
  • Economic and technical feasibility
  • Marketability of the product or service
Fees (indicative): one-time incubate registration ₹5,000 for TBI services. Physical cubicles: ₹1,500 per 3 months. Specialized equipment usage may attract nominal hourly charges.

Documents checklist

  • PAN / Aadhaar (submitted only via official application channels)
  • Executive summary of the idea
  • Permanent address proof
  • Two references
  • Company incorporation certificate, if any
  • Filled application with promoter photograph
  • Signed incubate–incubator agreement

Do not email sensitive ID documents to unofficial addresses. Use the university application form or hand documents to TBI as instructed.
